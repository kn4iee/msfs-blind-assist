READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS AIRCRAFT CAN DO TODAY

The A350 is being built section by section. Its overhead panel, its glareshield (the FCU
and both EFIS control panels) and its instrument panel (gear, autobrake, ISIS, source
switching, display power, both side and individual panels) are in the Sections list
(Ctrl+1): every switch, selector, guarded button and knob, with the panels named and
ordered as on the A380. A switch the aircraft did not move is said once ("BAT 1 did not
move"). Each panel's status display (Ctrl+3) reads its lights, the FCU's four windows,
each side's baro and the ISIS QNH setting as the cockpit shows them, and a button lit by
its own light reads On or Off on its row. The runway condition (Autobrake panel) is one
choice from 6 dry to 1 ice: the knob opens its menu on the SD, steps to it and closes it.

A knob whose value the cockpit shows is a typed field, as on the other Airbus aircraft: the
FCU Speed, Heading, Altitude and VS/FPA fields, each side's QNH (in that side's unit), the
ISIS QNH (hectopascals or inches) and the cabin altitude and V/S targets. Type the value and press Enter: the aircraft's own knob lands it
and the value the cockpit then shows is said ("FCU speed 250, selected"). A value the knob
cannot take is said instead (a Mach number while the speed window shows knots, a QNH while
that side is on STD).

The pedestal, the displays (PFD, ND, E/WD, SD, MFD), the OIS and the
aircraft's own hotkeys (FCU, autopilot, ECAM) follow, and this list grows with them. Until
then the hotkeys below are the ones every aircraft shares.

The A350 tunes its radios only through its radio management panels; the stock COM radio
the other aircraft use does not exist in it.


OUTPUT MODE - Press ] to activate

Location:
  C          Read Nearest City

Tools:
  Ctrl+H     Activate/deactivate Hand Fly mode
  Ctrl+V     Activate/deactivate Visual Guidance mode. Does not require Hand
             Fly mode. Enables the same single-letter quick-access hotkeys
             Hand Fly mode does, plus F for target VS.
  Ctrl+T     Activate/deactivate Takeoff Assistance
  Ctrl+D     Read Distance to Selected Runway
  Ctrl+I     Read ILS Guidance System Report

Taxi Guidance (turn-by-turn taxi assistance with audio steering tone):
  Y          Taxi Status (current taxiway, next turn, distance to destination)
  Ctrl+Y     Repeat Current Taxi Instruction
  Alt+Y      Where Am I (announces current taxiway, gate, or runway at nearest airport)
  Alt+L      Look Around (where you are, the apron or concourse you are in, and the nearest buildings with direction and distance)
  Ctrl+Shift+L  Surroundings window (everything nearby as a list, with the airport's fuel and frequencies)
                In its Frequencies list, Enter and Shift+Enter only tell you that this aircraft
                tunes its radios through its own radio management panels.
  (Open the form with Input mode > Shift+Y)

Waypoint Tracking (use Fix Track window or EFB route viewer to add):
  1          Track Slot 1
  2          Track Slot 2
  3          Track Slot 3
  4          Track Slot 4
  5          Track Slot 5

Traffic and Weather:
  R          Announce Tracked TCAS Traffic
  Ctrl+R     Open TCAS Traffic Window
  Shift+O    Open Volanta window (Volanta users and network pilots around you,
             your Volanta friends and online ATC; also Extras > Volanta)
  Shift+R    Open Weather Radar Window (auto-detects ActiveSky and silently
             uses richer data — surface wind+gust, ceiling, QNH, turbulence,
             METAR-decoded precipitation — when running. Falls back to MSFS
             SimVars when AS isn't running. Visibility shown in both km and
             statute miles either way.)
  Alt+G      Announce nearest ground traffic (up to 3 aircraft on ground with
             direction and distance; auto-alerts also fire at 500/250/100 ft
             zone transitions while taxiing)

Windows:
  Shift+E    Route viewer, procedure planner and airport lookup
  Shift+L    Location Info
  Shift+M    Show METAR Report (VATSIM, plus ActiveSky METAR if AS detected)
  Ctrl+Shift+T  Cold Temperature Altitude Correction (offline calculator: field elevation + temperature + published altitudes -> corrected altitudes)
  Shift+B    SimBrief Briefing

Hand Fly Mode Hotkeys:
  A          Read MSL
  S          Read IAS
  D          Read Distance to Destination Runway
  H          Read Magnetic Heading
  P          Read Aircraft Pitch
  B          Read Aircraft Bank
  Q          Read AGL
  V          Read VS

Visual Guidance Mode Hotkeys (same single-letter set as Hand Fly mode, plus F):
  A          Read MSL
  S          Read IAS
  D          Read Distance to Destination Runway
  H          Read Magnetic Heading
  P          Read Aircraft Pitch
  B          Read Aircraft Bank
  Q          Read AGL
  V          Read VS
  F          Read Target VS

Note: both modes share the quick-access hotkeys. Activate Hand Fly mode,
Visual Guidance mode, or both — the single-letter keys H, V, Q, S, D, B,
P, A work in either. F is Visual-Guidance-specific (announces "Visual
guidance not active" if pressed when VG is off).

Ground Services:
  Ctrl+G     Read latest GSX tooltip (no need to open the Access GSX window)

SayIntentions:
  Ctrl+S        Read last SayIntentions transmission
  Ctrl+Shift+S  Open SayIntentions flight information. Assigned arrival gate,
                departure runway, which runways are landing and departing, the
                field flow and the altimeter. Read-only: arrow keys move line by
                line, Escape closes.

VATSIM:
  Alt+V      Mute or unmute VATSIM announcements (vPilot)

INPUT MODE - Press [ to activate

Teleport:
  Shift+R    Runway Teleport
  Shift+G    Gate Teleport

Flight Planning:
  Shift+F    Fix/Waypoint Tracking Window
  Shift+D    Select Destination Runway

Taxi Guidance:
  Shift+Y    Open Taxi Assist Form (enter ATC taxi clearance, start guidance)
  Y          Continue past current hold-short point
  Ctrl+Y     Stop Taxi Guidance
  Shift+X    Open Landing Exit Planner (pick a runway exit taxiway before
             touchdown; guidance auto-starts when you land — reuses the ILS
             destination runway if one is set; announces headwind/tailwind on
             runway selection so you can pick an exit that matches the rollout)

Ground Services:
  Alt+G      Open Access GSX window (accessible GSX Ground Services Pro menu)

SayIntentions:
  Ctrl+Shift+Y Build a taxi route from the last SayIntentions taxi clearance.
               Fills the Taxi Assist form with the destination, taxiways and
               hold-short runways ATC gave, and names anything it could not
               apply. Review the fields and press Calculate Route to start
               guidance, or turn on auto-start in the SayIntentions section
               of Settings > Taxi Guidance.

General:
  Escape     Exit active hotkey mode
