READ THIS FIRST - WHAT THIS AIRCRAFT CAN DO TODAY

The A350 is being built section by section. Its overhead panel, its glareshield (the FCU
and both EFIS control panels), its instrument panel (gear, autobrake, the ISIS and the SND,
the standby navigation display, source switching, display power, both side and individual
panels) and its pedestal (engines, thrust levers, the three RMPs, flaps and brakes, speed
brake, trims, ECAM control panel, weather radar, transponder and cockpit) and the cockpit
around you (doors and hatches, sun shades, oxygen masks, HUD units, tillers and the
sidestick takeover buttons) are in the Sections list (Ctrl+1): every switch, selector,
guarded button and knob, with the panels named and ordered as on the A380, except that each
pilot's controls have panels of their own: a panel both pilots share on the A380 is split
into the shared part, then a "Captain ..." panel and a "First Officer ..." panel right after
it (Captain Wiper, First Officer Warnings, Captain ECAM Control Panel, Captain Weather
Radar, First Officer Oxygen Mask). A switch the aircraft did not move is said once ("BAT 1 did not
move"). Each panel's status display (Ctrl+3) reads its lights, the FCU's four windows,
each side's baro and the ISIS QNH setting as the cockpit shows them. A button shows its
state beside its name wherever the cockpit shows one: its light On or Off, its lit legends
("EXT 1: Available", "APU agent: Squib and discharged"), or what it switches ("SPD/MACH:
Knots", "Captain baro unit: Hectopascals", "Speed knob push: Managed"). On the A350 a
baro knob's push selects STD and its pull QNH (the A320's are the other way round). The runway
condition (Autobrake panel) is one choice from 6 dry to 1 ice: the knob opens its menu on
the SD, steps to it and closes it. The HUD is an option on the OIS options page: without it
the HUD panels' controls say "HUD is not fitted"; each side's declutter is an Off/On switch.
The SND is an option too (STANDBY NAV DISPLAY): without it its keys say "Standby navigation
display is not fitted".

Switches and lights speak when they change on their own ("Seat belts: On", "BAT 1 fault
light on", "Master caution on"), as the cockpit shows them; what you set yourself is not
repeated. Each one has its own row in the Monitor Manager (Ctrl+M), where you can mute it.

A knob whose value the cockpit shows is a typed field, as on the other Airbus aircraft: the
FCU Speed, Heading, Altitude and VS/FPA fields, each side's QNH (in that side's unit), the
ISIS QNH (hectopascals or inches) and the cabin altitude and V/S targets. Type the value and press Enter: the aircraft's own knob lands it
and the value the cockpit then shows is said ("FCU speed 250, selected"). A value the knob
cannot take is said instead (a Mach number while the speed window shows knots, a QNH while
that side is on STD).

The thrust levers, flaps and speed brake are combos with the A380's positions ("Thrust
lever 1: Climb", "Flaps: 2"), and they say where they went ("All thrust levers Climb",
"Spoilers 40 percent"). The rudder and pitch trims are nudge buttons, with the trim read
on their panel ("Pitch trim: 1.3 degrees up"). The System display page combo shows the SD
page and picks one.

The OIS, the A350's electronic flight bag, opens in a window like the PMDG tablets: the
captain's with Input mode > Shift+T, the first officer's with Input mode > Ctrl+Shift+T. Every
page reads in browse mode, from the FLT OPS menu to T.O PERF and LDG PERF (type, pick the
runway, COMPUTE, read the results), the loadsheet and refuel, charts, company messages,
GROUND (each door with its state, lights, chocks, pushback), MAINTENANCE, the options and, on
the preferred seat side's OIS, the Situation Manager with its failures (each Active or
Inactive). Its own messages, errors and confirmation questions are spoken as they appear.

The E/WD speaks on its own, window open or closed: each warning, caution, memo and procedure
line that appears is said with its colour, as the cockpit shows it ("APU EMER SHUTDOWN, amber",
"APU MASTER SW: OFF, cyan"), from power-up on. Ctrl+E turns these call-outs off and on; they are
also a row in the Monitor Manager (Ctrl+M). Alt+E opens the whole E/WD as a window: the engine
block, the warning display with the ECAM cursor ("selected") and each action box ("to do",
"done"), the SD page and its permanent data line. The first start after an A350 update says "A350
displays getting ready" while MSFSBA prepares them (about 15 seconds), then "ready".

The aircraft's own hotkeys are the A380's: the FCU read-outs and windows, the autopilot panel
(Ctrl+P), the knob pushes and pulls, the characteristic speeds the A350 publishes, fuel, weight,
flaps, gear, altimeter, squawk and each display read aloud. Each press drives the FCU panel's own
row, so a hotkey does exactly what that row does; what changed is said by the FMA call-out.

The A350 tunes its radios only through its radio management panels; the stock COM radio
the other aircraft use does not exist in it.


OUTPUT MODE - Press ] to activate

FCU Controls (each window as the FCU shows it, in the typed entries' words):
  Shift+H    Read FCU Heading ("FCU heading 293", "FCU heading dashes, managed")
  Shift+S    Read FCU Speed ("FCU speed 250 knots, managed", "FCU speed mach 0.85")
  Shift+A    Read FCU Altitude ("FCU altitude 40000 feet, managed")
  Shift+V    Read FCU V/S or FPA ("FCU vertical speed +1200 feet per minute")

Altitude:
  Q          Read Altitude AGL
  A          Read Altitude MSL

Airspeed:
  S          Read IAS (Indicated Airspeed)
  T          Read TAS (True Airspeed)
  G          Read Ground Speed
  M          Read Mach Number

Heading:
  H          Read Magnetic Heading
  U          Read True Heading

Navigation:
  Ctrl+W     Read the ND's TO waypoint (the captain's ND)
  D          Read the distance to destination along the route, and the time at the ground
             speed ("541 miles to destination: 01:16:44"; "not available" with no route)
  Shift+D    Read the distance to the top of descent ("443 miles to top of descent", "Top of
             descent not yet computed", "Past top of descent")

Characteristic Speeds (the values the A350 itself publishes):
  Shift+4    Read VLS (minimum selectable speed)
  Shift+5    Read the alpha protection and alpha max speeds (the A350's PFD shows no
             stall speed; its low-speed band starts there; "not shown" while it shows none)
  Shift+6    Read VFE Next (next flap limit)
  Shift+1    Read Green Dot speed  } the A350 shows these only as symbols on the speed tape:
  Shift+2    Read S speed          } read off the captain's PFD ("Green dot speed 217 knots";
  Shift+3    Read F speed          } "not shown" while the tape does not draw one; "more than
                                     50 knots below" while it sits off the tape's scale)

Fuel:

  Shift+F    Read Fuel On Board (kilograms)

Weight (the SD's own gross weight and centre of gravity):

Displays:
  Alt+P      Read the captain's PFD (the FMA first, then speeds, altitude, heading, messages)
  Alt+N      Read the captain's ND
  Alt+S      Read the SD page shown, with its values named
  Alt+I      Read the standby flight display
  Alt+E      Open the E/WD (Engine/Warning Display) window: engine block, every warning
             display line with its colour, the ECAM cursor and action boxes, the SD and its
             permanent data. Read with the arrow keys; auto-refreshes, F5 refreshes now,
             Escape closes.
  Ctrl+E     Toggle the E/WD auto call-outs (also in the Monitor Manager, Ctrl+M)

  Shift+M    Open the MFD window (A380 parity): the MFD page as lines (menus, buttons,
             tabs, fields with their values and colours, the scratchpad). Up/Down move;
             Enter acts on the line: a field takes what you typed in the scratchpad box
             (Tab to it, type, Enter), anything else is clicked. Backspace presses CLR,
             PageUp/PageDown scroll, F5 refreshes, Escape closes. The Side box picks the
             captain's or the first officer's MFD. A new scratchpad message is spoken.
             Alt+letter presses a KCCU page key: Alt+I INIT, Alt+F F-PLN, Alt+E PERF,
             Alt+L FUEL, Alt+R RAD NAV, Alt+P SEC, Alt+D DIR, Alt+A ATC, Alt+V SURV,
             Alt+C CLR, Alt+T DEST, Alt+G PROG, Alt+M MAILBOX, Alt+K C/L MENU,
             Alt+N NOTEPAD, Alt+O DATA, Alt+U MENU, Alt+B AIRPORT, Alt+Y REF, Alt+X ESC.

  The other displays have no hotkey window, as on the A380: open Displays in the Panels
  list (Ctrl+2): Captain and First Officer PFD (FMA, selected speed, altitude, selected
  altitude, QNH, radio altitude, heading, flags and messages), Captain and First Officer
  ND, Captain and First Officer MFD (read as drawn), FCU display, RMP 1 to 3 display,
  Standby flight display, Standby navigation display and BAT display. Each status
  display reads that display and refreshes every second. The FMA is spoken on every change
  with its colours, one Monitor Manager (Ctrl+M) row per FMA column.

Radios (the A380's keys):
  Ctrl+Shift+R  Open the RMP window: the chosen RMP's screen (each radio's active and
                standby frequency, the squawk) as lines, kept up to date. Control+1, 2
                or 3 picks the radio line. Type a frequency: the digits and the point go
                to the cockpit RMP's keypad as you type (the leading 1 and the point are
                optional: 2 1 9 is 121.900, 8 7 is 118.700); the first digit selects the
                line on the RMP, as the manual does. The RMP never shows what is typed,
                so Backspace deletes the last digit and says what is left. Enter loads
                the frequency as the line's standby and reads the line back ("standby
                unchanged" when the RMP refused it); Alt+1, 2 or 3 swaps that line's
                standby in and reads it back. A frequency typed and not loaded is
                deleted when you press another key ("272 cleared"). Alt+V, H, L, N, U
                press the VHF, HF, TEL, NAV and MENU page keys (the A350 draws only the
                VHF page: the others say "screen unchanged"). Alt+T, then four digits 0
                to 7: the squawk, set on the fourth digit (Escape cancels). The RMP box
                picks the Captain's RMP, the First Officer's or RMP 3; F5 refreshes,
                Escape closes.
                Every RMP key, microphone key, volume knob and reception (the knob pulled
                out) is also on the Pedestal's Captain RMP, First Officer RMP and RMP 3
                panels.
