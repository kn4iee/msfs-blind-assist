# First full flight on the branch: KATL to KDTW, 2026-10-31

Robin Kipp flew the iniBuilds A350-900 from KATL gate to KDTW gate on the Release build of `feat/inibuilds-a350` at
7e6d5fbd1 (deployed to `C:\MSFSBA`). The logs run from 14:47 to 18:24 local time with no gap; the app closed normally.
Excerpts below are copied from `%APPDATA%\MSFSBlindAssist\logs` on his machine (`a350_speech.log`, `a350_display.log`,
`debug.1.log` + `debug.log`). The OIS's Navigraph sign-in code, spoken at 14:50, is left out of this file on purpose.

Read from it: the two sides refused 30.5 in their unit (hectopascals) while the ISIS, which takes either unit by size,
went ahead. Right after the ISIS's short write and knob click, both sides left STD for QNH 1013, and the ISIS read
1013.25 instead of the value one step past its short write. The captain's unit button switched the first officer's

is what the OIS option LINKED INSTR (`INI_LINKED_INSTRUMENTS`) does by `docs/inibuilds-a350.md` ("Typed values": a click
runs `LinkBaros`, which copies the side to the other side and the ISIS), so the option was most likely on. Not
measured: which of the two ISIS writes moved the sides, and what a click does with the option off.

## The PARKING checklist at the gate

```text
18:21:51.064 announce: ENG 1 master: Off
18:21:52.054 announce: ENG 2 master: Off
18:21:57.056 announce: Beacon light: Off
18:22:05.036 announce: APU bleed: On
18:22:05.716 announce: Fuel pumps left on: not read on the ground, or an engine is not read stopped.
```

The refusal came 14 s after the masters went off, while both engines were still spinning down; it was right not to turn
the pumps off, but it names neither condition it found. It is a checklist tick's sentence (`FuelPumpsStayText`): the
Parking flow waits up to 120 s for the engines to read stopped before its pumps step, and the Fenix, A32NX, A330, A380
and iFly 737 ticks say the same words.
