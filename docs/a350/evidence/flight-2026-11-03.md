# Test flight, VCBI 04 to VCBI 04, 2026-11-03 (A350 1.2.8)

A 50-minute flight flown by the session's scripts through MSFSBA's own rows, windows and the probe's REPL (the MSFSBA definition
and display reader as MainForm runs them), for the in-flight items plans 07-16 left. Engines started at the stand by the First
Officer's flows; take-off on the guarded script; a PBD route to FL200 (KAT/360/25, /320/60, /260/70, /220/45), the top-of-descent
pause, a manual emergency descent, a direct-to, ACTIVATE APPR, an ILS Z 04 autoland with BTV, and the rollout. Every display's
draw was saved every 45 s (54 rounds, the corpus for decoding every sprite and shape); what MSFSBA said was collected every 5 s.
