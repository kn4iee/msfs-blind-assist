electronic checklist (C/L MENU, read 2026-11-03 on 1.2.8, ten checklists), and the native EFB checklists (`A35K.CHECKLISTS` in the
1.2.8 package), which the First Officer's read-back groups (`A350ChecklistDefinitions`, `*_CL`) follow line for line.
