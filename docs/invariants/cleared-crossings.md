# Cleared crossings: rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/cleared-crossings.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads `Navigation/ClearedCrossingHolds.cs` (`ClearedCrossingHolds`, `ClearedCrossingCallouts`), `Services/TaxiGuidanceManager.cs`, `Database/Models/TaxiRoute.cs` and the `ClearedCrossing*` tests. It does NOT load on `Services/TaxiGuidanceManager.Routing.cs`, which has no room left in its rule budget, so its call sites there are named here: `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses` (calls `ClearedCrossingHolds.Apply`), `TryRecalculateRoute` (`clearedStopMoves`), `PlaceTaxiwayHoldStop` (the event shift) and the two segment advances (`AdvanceSegment`, `AdvanceToNearestSegment`, which call `PassClearedLine`). Stage 2 of the Taxi UI Redesign (2026-11), from the design the owner approved on 2026-11-15 (sections 5, 8.3, 8.4 and 8.8). Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), "Cleared crossings"; measurements: [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md), "Cleared crossings".

## CLR-1

- **A runway stop comes off for one crossing at one place, and only because the pilot said so.** The automatic pass still places a stop before every runway on every route ([HLD-4]); `ClearedCrossingHolds.Apply` runs after it, inside `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses`, on the route as the Turn picks left it. Only `ClearedCrossingHolds` clears a runway stop, that is, takes one off for a clearance, from two inputs: a crossing the pilot entered (today Progressive Taxi's "After crossing runway": `LoadRoute(..., clearedCrossings:)`, with the cross-at taxiway as `At`, or a holding point's name as `At` and its node as `AtNodeId`), and the Continue key (CLR-3), whose early press and paired double press call `ClearAt` directly. Nothing else clears one, the design's first anti-goal: not a runway's name, not the SayIntentions fill, not a default, and not a recalculation, which only re-applies a clearance already given, at the same node (CLR-2). One older mechanism also removes runway stops and is not a clearance: the landed-runway vacate strip (`StripVacateHoldShortsOnLandedRunway`, in `TaxiGuidanceManager.Rollout.cs` and `.Backtrack.cs`), a backstop that drops a false "hold short" of the runway the aircraft has just landed on, only on the leading run, still on that runway, of a route leaving it after landing, so a pilot vacating is never told to stop on it. It predates this rule, is untouched by it, and never takes off a stop the route meets later, a re-crossing of that same runway included.

  **One place.** A request's candidates are the crossings of its runway (either end) still held by a stop whose own label names that runway. On the pilot's Calculate the match is taken in ROUTE order, where the route reaches the runway (`TaxiRouteRunwayEvent.ReachNodeIndex`), from EVERY crossing of the runway not yet cleared, passed ones and ones held by the pilot's own stop included, so a bare request is about the crossing in front of the aircraft, never a later one. With a place: first by `AtNodeId`, over the whole list before any name (a name an earlier crossing carries never takes the request from the crossing at the point): the first whose route from its stop node to where it reaches the runway, both included, passes it (the holding point the form pins the route through can sit between the scenery line the pass stopped at and the runway), or the first crossing whose stop lies at most 200 m along the route after it (`HoldingPointBeforeStopMaxMetres`) with no other runway event (stop or reach) between (a CAT II/III line further out than the CAT I line the pass stops at; further back, or at another stub of the same runway passed on a parallel taxiway, the point is somewhere else and that crossing is not cleared); then the first whose event location or stop taxiway is `At`. With a clearance row (`SeqIndex`), those the route reaches on or after that taxiway's run are tried first: the reach, not the stop, which can sit back on the taxiway before (the binding a pilot's runway pick uses, [HLD-5]). The first match is cleared when it is a candidate; one with no stop of its own (#355's decision 3) is marked cleared, so it is never said as having "no hold short point" ([HLD-17]); one already passed, or held by the pilot's own stop, keeps the request: nothing is cleared, nothing said, and no other crossing is tried. Before the final review a crossing that could not be cleared dropped out of the list, so a bare request on a twice-crossed runway cleared its SECOND crossing (and a row's request whose own crossing was the pilot's hold fell back to the first), the design's fourth anti-goal; `A_request_a_pilot_held_first_crossing_cannot_take_never_clears_the_second` and its passed and row twins pin it. A runway the route does not cross is said ("The route does not cross runway 04L."); one crossed only somewhere else keeps its stop and is said by the place the pilot named ("Runway 04L is not crossed at C. It is not cleared; you will be stopped there.", a holding point by its name, "... is not crossed at B3. ..."; "... is not crossed there. ..." only when there is no name to say); both ride in the summary and the must-hear list beside the Turn pick note (`LastClearedCrossingWarning`). Until stage 3 a holding point further OUT than the stop the pass chose was matched only by its name in the stop's label, so "cross 24L at B3" at a CAT II/III B3 was said as "not crossed at B3" and stopped there; `A_holding_point_further_out_than_the_stop_clears_the_crossing_it_leads_to` and `A_holding_points_node_is_matched_before_its_name` pin the change.

  **Never the pilot's own hold.** A stop shared with the pilot's own taxiway, holding-point or end-of-taxiway hold ("runway 09 at taxiway W") is never cleared (`CanClearLabel`): the pilot programmed that stop, and its countdown names the runway; a request it matches stops there, and a later crossing of the runway is never cleared in its place. A stop guarding two runways loses only the named one and holds for the other, which takes its own Continue ([HLD-11]).

  **Cleared, never deleted.** The design forbids a runway crossed in silence, and a pilot who cannot tell where a cleared runway's hold line is or cannot stop there when ATC takes the crossing back (the owner's concern of 2026-11-15). So `ClearAt` keeps the label the stop was placed with (`TaxiRouteSegment.PlacedHoldLabel`; a start hold, `TaxiRoute.PlacedStartHoldLabel`), lists the runway on `ClearedRunways` (`StartHoldClearedRunways`), and leaves its event `Held`, marked `Cleared`; with no runway left the segment is no longer a hold short. That label is what the countdown says, at the same distances as a stop and with no "Slow down" or "Stop" ("Runway 04L at C in 120 metres, cleared to cross.", then the distance alone: `CountsDownAsCleared`, `ClearedCrossingCallouts.Approach`; a leg end on the same segment speaks its own countdown instead); what the line says ("Crossing runway 04L at C.", `PassClearedLine`, wherever the segment cursor passes it, [STR-5]); and what the Hold short key puts back (`RestoreAt`, `RestoreStartHold`, which keep any runway a later pick added to the stop: `RestoredLabel`, CLR-3). The summary says "cleared across runway 04L" (`DescribeRunwayEvents`). A route that starts at a line every runway of which is cleared holds nowhere, and says the line once as the aircraft starts across (`AnnounceClearedStartCrossing`, `HasStartedAcross`: 2 kt, or more than 10 m along). The Continue that releases a stop cleared for one of its runways names every runway at it straight after "Continuing." ("Continuing. Crossing runway 09 and runway 01. Taxiway K.", `ClearedPartOfReleasedStop`). The Progressive strip this replaced (`StripClearedCrossing`, until 2026-11) deleted the stop and the runway's events, so an "After crossing runway" crossing got no countdown and no line.

  VirtualPilot (2026-11-25, fs2024, the 40 busiest airports): 364 flights flown with their first crossing cleared, 0 held at it; 341 of the 342 that reached it said its line once, at the line (the other, at ZSPD, heard the incursion watch repeat it off the route); 312 of 312 that were counted down said "cleared to cross" first; every other stop as with nothing cleared (364 of 364). `crossthenhold`, 50 legs: 0 held at the runway, 0 `LEG_ENDED_ON_RUNWAY`. ## CLR-2

- **A cleared crossing belongs to the stop it cleared, and is used once.** `ClearedCrossingHolds.Apply` returns each applied request with the node of the stop it cleared (`ClearedCrossing.StopNodeId`; 0 for a crossing with no stop) and the node where the route reaches the runway (`ReachNodeId`); a crossing with no stop also records the node before it, its near side (`NearNodeId`), which the step list pins a crossing made with no stop by (`ClearanceTrim`): the reach is the first node BEYOND the runway when one edge jumps across it, and a pin there read the far side as the near one. With no node before it on the route (the route started on the pavement, or the aircraft itself was the last clear point and its hop to the route's first node jumped the runway) the step is pinned at the reach and marked so (`ClearanceStep.AtNodeIsReach`): its side is never trusted, and the window refuses that leg. The manager keeps them in `_clearedCrossings`, with the early and paired Continue's own entries. Each says who cleared it (`ClearedCrossing.Origin`, `ClearedBy.Clearance` for a crossing the pilot entered, `ClearedBy.Continue` for a Continue's; both carry `SeqIndex` -1), kept through a recalculation, so stage 3 can tell a Cross step's crossing from one a key cleared. A crossing leaves that list where it is used: at its line (`PassClearedLine`, `UseClearedCrossing`, which keeps the entries it used for the Hold short key), at the Continue that releases a partly cleared stop, when the Hold short key puts its stop back, and, for a crossing with no stop of its own, which is never at a line, once the segment cursor reaches where the route reaches its runway (`WithoutPassedNoStop`, by `ReachNodeId`, on each taxiing frame, allocating nothing while there is none). The list is replaced, never mutated in place, because `LoadRoute`'s refusal snapshot and its rollback hold the reference ([RTE-29]): a refused Calculate leaves the route being flown cleared as it was. `StopGuidance` and the rollout reset empty it.

  **A recalculation re-clears only at that node** (the design's fourth anti-goal: a cleared crossing is never carried to another place). On a recalculation `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses` hands `Apply` the list, and only the candidate whose stop node is the recorded one is cleared again; anything else is dropped. A recalculated route that crosses the runway somewhere else, or a second time, stops there, and "Route changed" says so: "Runway 09L is a stop again." (`DroppedOnRecalculation`, for a crossing of that runway still held ahead). A crossing with no stop (node 0) is dropped too: re-clearing it could only go by runway name, which CLR-1 forbids, so its "no hold short point" warning can come back (ruled 2026-11-24: by then the aircraft is at or on that runway, and it is rare). A Calculate is a fresh statement: what the pilot entered applies to the new route as entered.

  **The [RTE-22] exception.** A recalculation that repeats the remaining taxiway names is normally discarded as a no-op. One that no longer reaches the stop of a crossing cleared at a stop still AHEAD on the route being replaced is adopted (`clearedStopMoves` in `TryRecalculateRoute`): keeping the old route would keep that stop cleared where the new route does not stop, and the pass then decides as above. A cleared stop already behind the aircraft is no reason; without "still ahead" a crossing just passed forced a spurious "Route changed".

  **Across a split.** A taxiway stop placed on a pilot's Calculate can split a segment ([TWH-2]); `TaxiwayHoldPlacement.ShiftEventsAfterSplit` moves every runway event's `StopNodeIndex` and `ReachNodeIndex` past it up by one, the honoured runway picks' events (built before the split) as well as the route's own, and `TaxiwayHoldPlacement.Split` keeps a stop's placed label and cleared runways on the far half, where the stop stays. Unshifted, a crossing is matched, and its stop cleared, at the wrong node (`A_split_keeps_an_events_stop_on_the_same_node`).

  VirtualPilot (2026-11-25): before a cleared stop, 24 recalculations that kept a Turn pick's tail, and 13 more on miss flights, cleared it again at its node. 22 flights lost the clearance: the new route stopped for that runway elsewhere and said "Runway 09L is a stop again." (16), no longer met it (3), crossed it with no stop and said so (2), or entered it, with a stop and a countdown (1, KLAX). None brought a stop back at the cleared node, cleared another crossing of the runway, or moved a crossing's stop without a word, except that last one: `DroppedOnRecalculation` speaks for a crossing only, so a runway that comes back as an ENTRY gets no "is a stop again" (left for the final review). ## CLR-3

- **No key but Continue clears a runway, and the Hold short key never does.** ATC can take a crossing back while the aircraft is on its way ("hold short of 04L, traffic on the runway"); a pilot who can see the hold line stops at it (the owner's concern of 2026-11-15). Input Alt+Y (`HoldShortOfNextClearedRunway`, deciding through the pure `ClearedCrossingHolds.DecideHoldShortKey`) puts a cleared stop back, however it was cleared, or reports; it never clears anything. A stop put back is no longer cleared (`RestoreAt` empties its `ClearedRunways`), so a second press passes over it: it puts back the next cleared crossing ahead if there is one, says "Past the hold line of …" for another cleared line said as passed within the minute, or else names the next stop, which can be the one just put back. In order: no route ("No taxi route."); a cleared start hold the aircraft has not started across; a cleared line already said as passed that the route still puts ahead (the segment jump can say a line up to about 100 m early), with the cursor back on its segment; the next cleared runway ahead, or the partly cleared stop the aircraft holds at, which then asks for every runway again, one Continue each; a cleared start hold the aircraft is still crossing, as past its line; a line said as passed, for a minute (`PastTheLineWindowSeconds`); the next stop of any kind ("Next stop taxiway L in 120 metres.", `FindNextStop`, the finder the taxi status readout uses too); else "No runway ahead on this route.". Restores come first, so the key never only reports while it can still put a stop back.

  **Never a stop on a runway.** A stop is put back only short of its line: past is more than `StopPassedToleranceMetres` beyond its node along the route, or within it either side and on the pavement of a runway the stop guards (`RouteRunwayCrossings.RunwayUnder`: a painted line can hug the pavement edge), or, off the route there, on that pavement at all. Past it the key says "Past the hold line of runway 04L at C." and changes nothing. A stop put back says "Holding short of runway 04L at C. 80 metres.", is counted down and held like any other (only its own countdown starts fresh, so another stop's last call never cuts the key's sentence off; while taxiing, the key's sentence stands for the countdown's outer call only within that call's distance, `RestoreSaysOuterCall` on the countdown's own `OuterCountdownFt`, so a stop put back further out still gets its heads-up and the first call is never "Slow down"), lifts the incursion watch's latch for its node, and raises `ClearedCrossingsChanged` for the planner window (nothing subscribes to it until stage 3). Its arguments (`ClearedCrossingsChangedEventArgs.Restored`) carry every crossing put back as recorded, its stop node and who cleared it, the ones a line said early had already used included (`RestoredCrossings`), so the window can tell it from another crossing of the same runway; it is raised while `_stateLock` is held, so a subscriber marshals to its own thread without blocking (BeginInvoke, never Invoke).

  **A stop put back keeps every runway at it.** A pilot's runway pick retried after the clearing (`RetryDeferredRunwayPicks` runs after the pass that clears, [HLD-16]) can share a cleared stop and add its own runway to the label. `RestoreAt` and `RestoreStartHold` put back the placed label plus each part the stop's label carries now that the placed one lacks (`RestoredLabel`, split as `RemainingLabel` splits, at " and runway ", by each part's first runway, either end), so that runway is still staged, one Continue each ([HLD-11]), and named at the line. Overwritten with the placed label, as it was until the final review, the pick's runway could be crossed after one Continue without being asked for (`Restoring_a_stop_a_later_pick_shares_asks_for_every_runway`).

  **Continue before the line** (design section 8.4). ATC often clears a crossing while the aircraft is still rolling. A Continue while taxiing clears the stop ahead only when `ClearedCrossingHolds.CanClearEarly` allows: the countdown has named that stop (any of its calls), so a stray key never clears a runway the pilot has not been told about; it is a hold short whose label names a runway; and it is not on the route's last segment (the departure runway's own hold, the end of a leg). It clears the first runway the label names through `ClearAt`, records it with its node like an entered crossing, and says "Cleared across runway 22R. No stop." or "Cleared across runway 09. Still holding short of runway 01.". A stop `ClearAt` refuses (shared with the pilot's own hold) stays a stop, and the press is silent, as it is before the countdown and while a paired offer waits for its window. A press made by mistake is undone with the Hold short key. The paired double-Continue for close runways (`PairedCrossingHoldWindow`) clears its stop the same way, and its offer scan stops at a stop `CanClearLabel` refuses, so a "cleared across both" press never meets a stop it cannot clear: blanking such a stop by hand once removed the pilot's own hold with no way back.

  VirtualPilot `--crossing-keys` (2026-11-25, fs2024, the 40 busiest airports, each plan's first crossing): Continue right after the first countdown call, 195 pressed, 195 "Cleared across runway R. No stop.", 0 held there, the line said once at the line in 195; the Hold short key right after the first "… cleared to cross.", 211 pressed, 211 put back and held there, none on runway pavement; the key 10.0 to 17.8 m past the line, 214 pressed, 214 "Past the hold line of …", 0 held there. The flights not pressed met their stop with no rolling approach (a stop segment under the 25 m capture radius, a route starting at the line) or lost it to a route change before it.

  Open, for the owner: a deliberate quick double Continue at a hold can clear the next stop's first runway when that stop lies within the countdown's outer call and is not offered as a pair; with two crossings cleared ahead, two Hold short presses put both back, however far apart in time (the safe side: each is spoken).
