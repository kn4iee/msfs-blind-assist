# Taxiway hold placement: rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/taxiway-holds.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md).

## TWH-1

- **A hold short of a taxiway stops before the taxiway, set back so the whole aircraft stays clear of it.** Both kinds go through one placement, `TaxiwayHoldPlacement.Choose`, called from `TaxiGuidanceManager.PlaceTaxiwayHoldStop`: a row's **Hold short of runway** list set to a taxiway ("via A, hold short of taxiway B", `ApplyUserTaxiwayHoldShorts`) and a Progressive Taxi leg that ends "hold short of taxiway B" (`ApplyLegEndSetback`). Until stage 1 of the Taxi UI Redesign both stopped AT the junction node, which puts the nose on the taxiway; at KCLE 2026-11-14 ("P, hold short of taxiway L") the pilot stopped 21.0 m short of the junction without being told to. The setback is `SetbackMetres(wingspan)`: `JunctionHalfWidthM` (15 m, half a wide taxiway plus a margin) plus `NoseShareOfWingspan` (0.55) of the wingspan, `DefaultWingspanM` (36 m) when it is unknown, clamped to `MinSetbackM` and `MaxSetbackM` (25 to 60 m): 34.8 m for a 36 m wingspan. The form sets `AircraftWingspanMetres` before each Calculate.

  The stop, in order:
  1. An existing route node at least the setback before the junction (less `NodeMatchToleranceM`, 1 m) and at most `ExistingNodeReachM` (40 m) beyond it.
  2. On a pilot's Calculate only, a node inserted on the route's own edge exactly the setback back (`TaxiGraph.InsertHoldingPointNodeOnEdge`, kept only when the edge it split is that segment's own; otherwise the junction, logged). Never within `MinFromRouteStartM` (10 m) of the route's start, and never on an off-chart or uncharted segment (TWH-2).
  3. Only without inserting (a recalculation, or the setback point on an off-chart or uncharted leg), an existing node up to `RecalcMaxBackM` (150 m) back. A recalculation never inserts a node ([RTE-30]); the 1 m tolerance lets it find the node inserted at load, measured from the split edges.
  4. Otherwise the junction itself: a route shorter than the setback, no node in reach, a setback point within 10 m of the route start, or an insert that failed.

  **Runways.** A candidate (a node or an inserted point) is usable only when the whole route from `ProgressiveLegEnd.CaptureRadiusM` (25 m) BEFORE it up to the junction is clear of every runway's margin (`RouteRunwayCrossings.RunwayWithinClearMargin`, the margin the automatic runway pass demands of a stop it invents), sampled at most `SampleStepM` (5 m) apart along each segment, not only at its nodes. Where that stretch runs off the start of the route, only the part that exists is judged. Why the 25 m: a mid-route hold is captured `WAYPOINT_CAPTURE_RADIUS_M` (25 m, the same value, `LegEndWiringTests` pins it) before its stop, and a leg end at `CaptureRadiusM`; the plan's first rule checked only the stretch from the stop on, so a stop moved forward to a node 5 m past a runway's margin was captured, and a leg ended, with the aircraft still on the pavement, with "Slow down" and "Stop." spoken there (whole-stage review, Critical 1; spec 8.5, 8.8). VirtualPilot's `legends` found two such legs, "Slow down" said on the pavement (RKSI "via C9, hold short of B3", KLSV "via A, hold short of F"), and none after the fix. Sampling, not nodes alone: a runway crossed inside one edge has no node on it, and each of rules 1 to 3 was caught in review landing behind such a runway (`Rule_1_never_lands_behind_a_runway_crossed_inside_one_edge` and its two siblings). A refused candidate moves forward, to the first node whose 25 m approach is clear, else to the junction; it never moves back. A junction inside a margin, or whose own 25 m approach is not clear, gets no stop (no point before it can be clear either): a row pick says so in the summary ("Note: no place to hold short of taxiway B clear of the runway; no stop will be called there.") and the runway's own automatic stop still stands before that runway; a leg end keeps the junction as built, and never ends on runway pavement (STR-25). Such a junction or first node past a runway can leave the nose of a large aircraft on the taxiway: the stop is close to it by necessity (KLSV "via A, hold short of F": the stop is the F junction, 03L lies just before it, and the aircraft stood 18.4 m from it).

  **The leg end.** On a pilot's Calculate a "hold short of taxiway B" leg is first cut to the FIRST meeting with B along the route from the last row's taxiway that is clear of every runway's margin, which a Turn pick on an earlier row can have moved, then set back. A meeting inside a margin is skipped, not refused: the route then passes through B before the leg holds short of a later meeting, and nothing is said (whether to refuse or add a note instead is the owner's call). A row pick, by contrast, is judged at its first meeting only. The junction stays `_destinationNodeId`, so a recalculation that routes afresh reaches it and is cut back again, without inserting (TWH-2 for a kept tail). Where the form finds the junction: STR-25.

  **The tick-box stop keeps its place.** The row's **Hold short** tick box ("Hold short of end of taxiway A", `ApplyUserHoldShorts`) is not this placement: it stays at the end of the row's taxiway, where it meets the next one, with its own label (HLD-2).

  **Unconfirmed.** `JunctionHalfWidthM`, `NoseShareOfWingspan`, `DefaultWingspanM`, the two clamps and `ExistingNodeReachM` are assumptions the owner has not yet confirmed in the sim. The only measurement so far is VirtualPilot's `legends` mode (2026-11-24, 40 airports, fs2024 navdata, a 36 m wingspan; docs/virtual-pilot.md, "Leg ends and taxiway holds"; figures after the review fixes): the stop at load lay a median 37.3 m before the junction without Turn picks (0 to 72.8 m: KLSV's is the junction, every other 33.9 m or more) and 35.5 m with them; the aircraft stood a median 54.5 m before the junction without picks (min 18.4, p90 69.5) and 56.5 m with them (min 16.4, p90 75.5), never at or past it; 83 legs stood more than 60 m back (an existing node up to 40 m beyond the setback, then the 25 m capture). Re-measure with it, and ask the owner, before changing a constant. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## TWH-2

- **A taxiway stop is placed on the route the Turn picks leave, and keeps their turns.** In `LoadRoute`, `ApplyLegEndSetback` runs after `TurnPickApplier.Apply` and before the pilot's own hold-short picks, and `ApplyUserTaxiwayHoldShorts` runs among those picks (`LegEndWiringTests`): a pick can replace the route and move the junction (TURN-1), so a stop placed first would be on a route then thrown away. On a recalculation the setback runs before the sanity gate, so the gate compares two cut routes. Each clause exists because its absence broke something:
  - **It never splits an off-chart leg.** An off-chart or uncharted segment (`IsOffChartTurn`, `IsUncharted`) has no graph edge to insert a node on. `Choose` refuses rule 2 there and takes an existing node up to 150 m back, or the junction (`An_off_chart_leg_is_never_split`).
  - **It never moves back before the picked turn.** The pick's `FirstTaxiwaySegment` on the stop's row (`firstUsableIndex`) floors the stop: a stop before the turn cut the turn off the route while the load note still announced it ("Turning left onto taxiway A, as picked."). When the turn lies closer to the taxiway than the setback, the stop is the first node on the picked taxiway, or the junction (`A_turn_too_close_to_the_taxiway_keeps_the_junction`). In VirtualPilot's `legends` run eight picked legs stood 16 to 23 m before the junction, for this reason or because the setback point fell on an off-chart leg. A Turn pick whose turn still lies past a leg end's stop is dropped from the route and logged.
  - **Every split shifts the indices.** A split turns segment m into two, so every Turn pick's `FirstSegmentAfterDeparture` and `FirstTaxiwaySegment` past m moves up by one (`ShiftPicksAfterSplit`), and so does every deferred runway pick's `RunStart` (retried after adoption, HLD-16). Left unshifted, the off-chart sentence, the missed-turn call and a kept-tail recalculation all read the wrong segment.
  - **A kept tail is never cut again.** A recalculation that keeps a Turn pick's loaded turn and tail (`TurnPickCarryOver.TailFrom`, TURN-3) ends at the stop placed at load, and `TryRecalculateRoute` skips `ApplyLegEndSetback` for it (`keepsLoadedTail`). The end-node test alone cut such a tail again whenever its stop was the junction itself, and the second cut moved the stop back again.

  VirtualPilot `legends` (2026-11-24): 42 picks changed the route; no `LEGEND_PICK_INDEX`, `LEGEND_OFFCHART_SPLIT`, `LEGEND_ENDS_OFFCHART` or `LEGEND_PICK_NOT_FIRST_JUNCTION` finding, and with a pick applied the stop never moved back before the turn. Without a pick to hold it, the stop can move back before the turn onto the last taxiway: 46 of the 56 refused picks' routes ended on the taxiway before it, the junction lying less than the setback past the turn (KLSV "E, F"); whether a stop must stay on the taxiway being followed is the owner's call. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)
