combo [Option 1] {Option 1|Option 2}  VNAV takeoff behavior
pre
    VNAV IS NOT DEPENDENT on CLB thrust for engagement.
    CLB thrust is selected automatically when VNAV engages. Arming VNAV on
    the ground can therefore result in CLB thrust being selected at 400 ft RA.
    
    CAUTION: ARMING BEHAVIOR ON GROUND MAY RESULT IN UNDESIRED
    THRUST SETTING during takeoff and initial climb segments.
    
    For both options, VNAV engagement requires at least 400 ft RA,
    and acceleration follows FMC ACCEL HT.
