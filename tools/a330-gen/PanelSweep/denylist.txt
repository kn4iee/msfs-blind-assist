# PanelSweep deny list: the A330 controls the sweep NEVER writes.
#
# One control a line: <control key> TAB <what writing it would do>. '#' starts a comment line; blank
# lines are ignored. Keys are the keys of MSFSBlindAssist/Resources/a330_control_map.json, or of the
# definition's own controls (IniA330Definition.OwnPlacedControls). A line naming no control would
# silently deny nothing, so a test fails on one. Parsed by Sweep/DenyList.cs.
#
# The planner also skips a switch whose guard is denied, and never sweeps a guard row on its own. A
# denied control is tried only by hand, one at a time, with the owner's go-ahead (plan 04, Task 4 Step 4).
# Remove a line only on purpose.

# Engine masters, the start selector and the manual starts: start or stop an engine
A330_ENG1_MASTER	starts or shuts down engine 1
A330_ENG2_MASTER	starts or shuts down engine 2
A330_ENG_MODE	puts the engine start selector to crank or ignition and begins a start sequence
A330_MAN_START1	cranks engine 1 by hand
A330_MAN_START2	cranks engine 2 by hand

# Fire handles, agents and tests: discharge a bottle or cut an engine's supplies
A330_ENG1_FIRE	pulling the handle shuts engine 1's fuel, hydraulics, bleed and generator
A330_ENG2_FIRE	pulling the handle shuts engine 2's fuel, hydraulics, bleed and generator
A330_APU_FIRE	pulling the handle shuts the APU down
A330_ENG1_AGENT1	discharges engine 1's first fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_ENG1_AGENT2	discharges engine 1's second fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_ENG2_AGENT1	discharges engine 2's first fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_ENG2_AGENT2	discharges engine 2's second fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_APU_AGENT	discharges the APU's fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_ENG_FIRE_TEST	sounds the fire warnings and the master warning
A330_APU_FIRE_TEST	sounds the APU fire warning and the master warning
A330_CARGO_SMOKE_TEST	sounds the cargo smoke warnings and the master warning
A330_FWD_CARGO_AGENT	discharges the forward cargo fire bottle, which cannot be recharged
A330_AFT_CARGO_AGENT	discharges the aft cargo fire bottle, which cannot be recharged

# IDG disconnects and the RAT: cannot be undone in flight or from the cockpit
A330_IDG1	disconnects generator 1's drive, which only maintenance can reconnect
A330_IDG2	disconnects generator 2's drive, which only maintenance can reconnect
A330_RAT	deploys the ram air turbine, which only maintenance can stow

# The ADIRS selectors and IR push-buttons: switching one off loses its alignment (measured 2026-11-19: input 1 on each put all three in NAV)
A330_IRS1_MODE	realigns inertial reference 1, minutes with the aircraft still
A330_IRS2_MODE	realigns inertial reference 2, minutes with the aircraft still
A330_IRS3_MODE	realigns inertial reference 3, minutes with the aircraft still
A330_IR1	switching IR 1 off loses its alignment and realigns it
A330_IR2	switching IR 2 off loses its alignment and realigns it
A330_IR3	switching IR 3 off loses its alignment and realigns it

# Batteries and external power: the cockpit goes dark
A330_BAT1	the cockpit goes dark when battery 1 is off with no other source
A330_BAT2	the cockpit goes dark when battery 2 is off with no other source
A330_APU_BAT	the APU's battery going off stops an APU start and its own supplies
A330_EXT_A	external power A off blacks out the cockpit on the ground
A330_EXT_B	external power B off blacks out the cockpit on the ground

# The APU: changes the bleed and electrical state
A330_APU_MASTER	starts or stops the APU and changes the bleed and electrical state
A330_APU_START	starts the APU

# Emergency and safety systems that affect the cabin or the aircraft
A330_DITCHING	closes the outflow valve and the openings below the waterline
A330_EMER_EXIT_LT	turns on the emergency exit lights, which run down the emergency battery

# Calls: ring the cabin crew or the ground crew
A330_CALL_ALL	calls the whole cabin crew
A330_CALL_FWD	calls the forward cabin crew
A330_CALL_MID	calls the mid cabin crew
A330_CALL_AFT	calls the aft cabin crew
A330_CALL_PURS	calls the purser
A330_CALL_EXIT	calls the cabin crew to the exit
A330_MECH_CALL	calls the ground mechanic

# The definition's own controls (not in the map): they move the aircraft or its surfaces
A330_GEAR	moves the landing gear
A330_FLAPS	moves the flaps and slats
A330_SPDBRK	moves the speedbrake lever
A330_SPLR_ARM	arms or disarms the ground spoilers
A330_PARKBRK	sets or releases the parking brake, and the aircraft may roll
